# Using a credit card on UPI (RuPay)

## Which cards work on UPI
Only RuPay credit cards can be linked to UPI apps in India. Visa, Mastercard, Amex and Diners credit cards cannot. Once linked, you can scan any merchant QR code at a kirana, chemist or petrol pump and pay from your credit limit instead of your bank account. You cannot use a credit card on UPI to send money to a person (person-to-person transfers), only to pay merchants.

## How to link a RuPay credit card to UPI
1. Open a UPI app (BHIM, Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm, your bank's app, or the card's own app such as Kiwi or super.money).
2. Choose "Add RuPay credit card". Your mobile number in the UPI app must be the one registered with the card.
3. Enter the last six digits and the expiry date, then verify with the OTP sent by your bank.
4. Set a separate UPI PIN for the credit card. It is not the same as the card PIN.
Never share the OTP or UPI PIN with anyone, including callers who say they are from the bank.

## Do UPI payments earn rewards?
It depends on the card. Small UPI payments (up to ₹2,000) carry no fee for the merchant, so many banks pay little or no reward on them. Read your card's terms. What some banks say on their own pages (checked 2026-10-20):
- Kiwi (YES Bank and PNB): 1.5% cashback on UPI scan and pay through the Kiwi app, 0.5% on online spends.
- super.money superCard (Utkarsh SFB, FD-backed): 1% cashback on UPI and other spends, UPI cashback capped at ₹500 a month.
- Axis Bank SUPERMONEY: 1% on other eligible spends including UPI; 3% on UPI through the super.money app, but that 3% is capped at the cashback earned from other spends in the same cycle.
- IDFC FIRST EA₹N (FD-backed): 1% cashback on UPI through the IDFC FIRST app, 0.5% through other UPI apps.
- IDFC FIRST Hello Cashback: 1% cashback on UPI through the IDFC FIRST app; UPI through other apps earns nothing.
- HDFC Bank Tata Neu Plus: 0.25% NeuCoins on UPI, up to 1% with a Tata Neu UPI ID; Tata Neu Infinity up to 1.5%. UPI NeuCoins capped at 500 a month.
- SBI Card Tata Neu Plus: 1% NeuCoins on other spends including UPI; Tata Neu Infinity 1.5%.
- HDFC Bank UPI RuPay: CashPoints on UPI at the card's normal rates. Given only to existing HDFC Bank cardholders.
- AU InstaPay: 1% cashback on UPI at departmental stores, dining, pharmacy and grocery, capped at ₹100 a statement cycle.
WalletWise counts UPI spending only on RuPay cards, and uses these checked rates where it has them.

## The risk: UPI makes overspending easy
Paying by UPI on a credit card feels like spending your own money, but it is borrowed money. Many small payments add up fast, and the bill arrives a month later. Research on rewards shows people spend more when paying feels painless. Before you link a card to UPI:
- Set a monthly budget and check the card app every week.
- Use a low credit limit if you are new to credit.
- Pay the total amount due every month. Interest of about 3 to 4% a month wipes out any 1% cashback.
- If you often pay only the minimum, do not link a credit card to UPI. Use your debit card or bank UPI instead.
