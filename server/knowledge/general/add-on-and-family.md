# Add-on and family cards (India)

## What an add-on card is
An add-on card is a supplementary card on the main (primary) cardholder's account, issued to someone with a relationship the bank accepts, such as a spouse, parent, sibling or child over 18. It works within the main card's credit limit: it does not get a separate limit (para 5(1)). Each bank decides who can get one and how many add-on cards an account can have.

## Who pays the bill
The liability for all dues, including what add-on cards spend, rests with the main cardholder, not the add-on cardholder (para 18). Interest and late fees on unpaid add-on spends are charged to the main cardholder's account.

## Fees and benefits
Many banks issue add-on cards free, but some charge a joining or annual fee for them; the Key Fact Statement and the bank's schedule of charges show it. Benefits differ by card. Lounge access for add-on cards varies: some cards let add-on members use the main card's lounge visits, some give add-on members their own visits, and some give them none. Check the card's Benefits tab in WalletWise and confirm with the bank before you travel.

## Sharing a card safely with family
- Give a family member their own add-on card instead of lending your card or sharing its details.
- Set a spending limit on the add-on card in the bank's app if the bank offers one.
- Never share your OTP, CVV or PIN, even with family. An OTP approves a payment from your account.
- Turn on SMS or app alerts so you see every add-on transaction.
- Pay the full bill every month, so add-on spends never cost you interest.

## Add-on cards and credit history
An add-on card can help a family member with no credit card of their own, such as a student, homemaker or retired parent, get used to a card. It does not always build their own credit score, because banks report add-on cards in different ways. Ask the bank whether it reports the add-on card to credit bureaus in the add-on holder's name.

## Pooling reward points
Some banks let family members combine or transfer reward points between their own cards, and others do not. Rules on pooling points change often and are not in WalletWise's card data, so ask the bank before planning a redemption around pooled points. Points earned on add-on cards usually go to the main card's account.

## Sharing your WalletWise wallet
WalletWise has no account or server. To copy your wallet to another phone, open Family and add-on cards in Learn and use Share my wallet: it makes a text code with your card names and points balances (last 4 digits only if you choose). It never includes full card numbers, CVV, PIN or expiry dates. Paste the code on the other phone to add the same cards there.
