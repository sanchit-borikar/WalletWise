# SBI Cashback Credit Card

Also called: SBI Cashback, Cashback SBI Card. Issued by SBI Card on the Visa network. Tier: mid.

## Fees and eligibility
- Joining fee ₹999 + GST; annual fee ₹999 + GST.
- Annual fee waived on ₹2,00,000 spend in a year.
- Forex markup 3.5% + GST.
- Interest on unpaid balance: 3.75% a month.

## Reward rates
Cashback is credited to the card statement (₹1 = ₹1).
- Online spends: 5% cashback, capped at ₹2,000 of cashback a month (5% stops after ₹40,000 of online spend).
- Offline spends: 1% cashback, capped at ₹2,000 a month.
- Combined cap: ₹4,000 of cashback per statement cycle since 1 April 2026.

## Lounge access
No airport lounge access is listed.

## Redemption
Cashback is credited automatically to the statement; there are no points to transfer.

## Perks
1% fuel surcharge waiver.

## Exclusions and terms
The 1 April 2026 change added new exclusions alongside the caps. Research notes (secondary sources) list gaming and some utility and government payments as excluded. Check SBI Card's current terms.

## Recent changes
- 1 April 2026 (major cut): ₹4,000 combined cashback cap per cycle (₹2,000 online + ₹2,000 offline), with sub-caps and new exclusions.
