# HDFC Bank Infinia Metal Credit Card

Also called: HDFC Infinia, Infinia Metal. Issued by HDFC Bank on the Visa network. Tier: super premium.

## Fees and eligibility
- Joining fee ₹12,500 + GST; annual fee ₹12,500 + GST.
- Annual fee waived on ₹10,00,000 spend in a year.
- Forex markup 2% + GST.
- Interest on unpaid balance: 1.99% a month.
- No income minimum is listed in the data.
- From 1 April 2026, keeping the card requires ₹18,00,000 of card spend a year or a ₹50,00,000 banking relationship.

## Reward rates
Rates are value back as a percentage of spend, valuing one HDFC Reward Point at about ₹1.
- Everyday spends: 3.3%.
- Groceries: 3.3%, capped at ₹2,000 of reward value a month.
- Utility bills: 3.3%, capped at ₹2,000 a month.
- Insurance: 3.3%, capped at ₹10,000 a month.
- HDFC SmartBuy travel bookings: up to 16.5%, capped at ₹15,000 a month.
- HDFC SmartBuy brand vouchers (GyFTR, Woohoo): up to 16.5%, capped at 3,000 reward points a month since 1 July 2026.

## Lounge access
Unlimited domestic and international airport lounge visits through Priority Pass.

## Redemption and transfer partners
- HDFC Reward Points are worth about ₹1 each on the default route (SmartBuy flights and hotels).
- Transfer partners include Singapore Airlines KrisFlyer and Air France-KLM Flying Blue (1:1 or 2:1 depending on card), Accor ALL (2:1), Air India Maharaja Club, Qatar Privilege Club and British Airways Executive Club (2:1 or 3:1 depending on card), Club ITC (2:1) and Marriott Bonvoy (2:1 or 3:1).
- In January 2026 Turkish Miles&Smiles and Avianca LifeMiles moved from 1:1 to 2:1.

## Perks
Concierge, golf benefits, hotel membership benefits and complimentary insurance cover.

## Exclusions and terms
This dataset does not list detailed category exclusions. Check the card's current terms (MITC) on the HDFC website for fuel, rent, wallet and government payments.

## Recent changes
- 1 July 2026 (major cut): SmartBuy brand-voucher earning capped at 3,000 reward points a month.
- 15 May 2026 (minor cut): dynamic currency conversion markup raised to 1.75%.
- 1 April 2026 (major cut): retention requires ₹18 lakh card spend a year or a ₹50 lakh banking relationship.
