# HDFC Diners Club Black Metal Edition

Also called: Diners Club Black, Diners Black, HDFC Diners Black Metal, Diners Black International. It is issued by HDFC Bank on the Diners Club International network. Tier: super premium.

## Fees and eligibility
- Joining fee ₹10,000 + GST; annual fee ₹10,000 + GST.
- Annual fee waived on ₹8,00,000 spend in a year.
- Forex markup 2% + GST on foreign-currency spends.
- Interest on unpaid balance: 1.99% a month.
- Stated minimum income: ₹2,50,000 a month. Age 21 to 60.

## Reward rates
Rates are value back as a percentage of spend, valuing one HDFC Reward Point at about ₹1 (its typical redemption value).
- Everyday spends: 3.33%, with earning capped at ₹75,000 of reward value a month.
- Dining: 4.33%.
- HDFC SmartBuy travel bookings: up to 33.33%, capped at ₹10,000 of reward value a month.
- HDFC SmartBuy online shopping: up to 16.5%, capped at ₹10,000 a month.
- HDFC SmartBuy brand vouchers (GyFTR, Woohoo): up to 10%, capped at 3,000 reward points a month since 1 July 2026.

## Lounge access
Unlimited domestic and international airport lounge visits through Priority Pass and the Diners Club lounge programme. No spend condition is listed in the data.

## Redemption and transfer partners
- Points earn HDFC Reward Points, worth about ₹1 each on the default route (SmartBuy flights and hotels).
- Points can be transferred to airline and hotel programmes including Singapore Airlines KrisFlyer and Air France-KLM Flying Blue (1:1 or 2:1 depending on card), Accor ALL (2:1), Air India Maharaja Club, Qatar Privilege Club, British Airways Executive Club and Etihad Guest (2:1 or 3:1 depending on card), Club ITC (2:1), and Marriott Bonvoy (2:1 or 3:1).
- In January 2026 Turkish Miles&Smiles and Avianca LifeMiles moved from 1:1 to 2:1 for this card.
- Transfers cannot be reversed. Confirm the current ratio in the bank portal before transferring.

## Perks
Concierge, golf benefits, hotel membership benefits, complimentary insurance cover and milestone bonuses on yearly spend.

## Exclusions and terms
This dataset does not list detailed category exclusions for this card. Check the card's current terms (MITC) on the HDFC website for categories such as fuel, rent and wallet loads.

## Recent changes
- 1 July 2026 (major cut): SmartBuy brand-voucher earning capped at 3,000 reward points a month.
- 15 May 2026 (minor cut): dynamic currency conversion markup raised to 1.75%.
