# HDFC Millennia Credit Card

Also called: HDFC Millennia, Millennia. Issued by HDFC Bank on the Visa network. Tier: mid. (Different from the IDFC FIRST Millennia card.)

## Fees and eligibility
- Joining fee ₹1,000 + GST; annual fee ₹1,000 + GST.
- Annual fee waived on ₹1,00,000 spend in a year.
- Forex markup 3.5% + GST.
- Interest on unpaid balance: 3.75% a month.
- Stated minimum income: ₹35,000 a month. Age 21 to 40.

## Reward rates
Cashback, valued at ₹1 per unit.
- 5% on Amazon, BookMyShow, Cult.fit, Flipkart, Myntra, SonyLIV, Swiggy, Tata CLiQ, Uber and Zomato, capped at ₹1,000 a month.
- 1% on other spends, capped at ₹1,000 a month.

## Lounge access
4 domestic airport lounge visits a year, unlocked by ₹1,00,000 of spend in the previous quarter.

## Redemption
Cashback is redeemed against the card statement.

## Perks
1% fuel surcharge waiver and milestone benefits on yearly spend.

## Exclusions and terms
This dataset does not list detailed exclusions. Check HDFC's current terms (MITC) for fuel, rent, wallet and government payments.

## Recent changes
- 15 May 2026 (minor cut): dynamic currency conversion markup raised to 1.75%.
