# Axis Bank Atlas Credit Card

Also called: Axis Atlas. Issued by Axis Bank on the Mastercard network. Tier: premium. Earns Axis EDGE Miles.

## Fees and eligibility
- Joining fee ₹5,000 + GST; annual fee ₹5,000 + GST. No spend-based fee waiver is listed.
- Forex markup 3.5% + GST.
- Interest on unpaid balance: 3.75% a month.
- Stated minimum income: ₹1,00,000 a month. Age 18 to 70.

## Reward rates
Rates are value back as a percentage of spend, valuing one EDGE Mile at about ₹2 (typical redemption value).
- Everyday spends: 4%.
- Travel booked on the Axis Travel EDGE portal or directly with airlines and hotels: 10%, capped at ₹20,000 of reward value a month.

## Lounge access
The data lists airport lounge access through Priority Pass but not the number of visits; check Axis terms.

## Redemption and transfer partners
- EDGE Miles transfer to many airline and hotel programmes, including Singapore Airlines KrisFlyer, Air France-KLM Flying Blue, Air India Maharaja Club, Qatar Privilege Club, British Airways Executive Club, Accor ALL, Asia Miles and Hilton Honors.
- Ratios vary by card tier (1:4 to 2:1). Partners are split into Group A and Group B with yearly transfer caps.
- 22 September 2026: Flying Blue, Air India and Qantas moved into the smaller Group A cap; Club ITC was removed; Accor ALL returned (Group A); Asia Miles, Lufthansa Miles & More and Hilton Honors were added.
- 5 August 2026: Qatar Privilege Club returned at 2 EDGE Miles : 1 Avios on the Atlas.

## Perks
Milestone bonuses on yearly spend and lounge access.

## Exclusions and terms
From 28 August 2026, tolls and road fees no longer earn rewards and gift cards are treated as their own category. Check Axis terms (MITC) for other exclusions.

## Recent changes
- 22 September 2026 (major cut): Flying Blue, Air India and Qantas moved into the smaller Group A transfer cap.
- 22 September 2026 (minor cut): Club ITC removed as a transfer partner.
- 22 September 2026 (improvement): Accor ALL returns; Asia Miles, Miles & More and Hilton Honors added.
- 28 August 2026 (minor cuts): tolls stop earning; late-payment fee of ₹1,300 above ₹50,000 outstanding; dynamic currency conversion markup 3.5% + GST.
- 5 August 2026 (improvement): Qatar Privilege Club returns as a transfer partner.
